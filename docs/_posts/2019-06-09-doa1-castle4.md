### Dark Lord encounter

The final boss fight of _Dungeons of Avalon_ is practically impossible.
This is due to a combination of extreme enemy difficulty and the
[fixed-encounter records bug](../game/bugs.html#victory-conditions-dont-trigger),
where the game forgets enemies killed when leaving the battle, meaning you have
to defeat them all in one go.
If you somehow do win, the same bug also prevents the game from registering the
victory conditions needed to trigger the
[final cutscene](../secrets/doa1-ending.html).

The advice is to fix the bug. Even so, the encounter. Some strategy advice:

- As a fixed encounter, all enemies start at distance 0, and can attack in the
  first turn. They take their turns either before (on 75% of turns) or after
  (25%) the first, second, and third hero's turn, respectively. Each has a 50-50
  chance to use their spell, or make a regular attack.
- The Anti-Aura scroll persists even if you flee (43.75% chance to flee
  successfully) or save and re-load the game. It may be wise to have the first
  hero use the scroll, flee, heal up, and return, so save the first hero's turn.
- In total, the Dark Lord has a 75% chance to target a hero in the front row and
  a 25% chance to target the second row, in both cases an instant kill. However,
  there are two defences:
  - A Magician can cast Magic Armour before battle for +3 to WC. Combined with
    the best possible [item](../game/items-and-treasure.html) loadout, and a
    Level 16 Fighter, Knight, or Hunter with at least 80 Luck, two heroes
    (ideally your front row) can reach WC 17, which, when using the Parry
    option (+3), is just enough to block both the Dark Lord's attacks.
    A Wizard can only cast Magic Armour2 for +2 WC, and the item situation is
    that only one hero can reach WC 17 this way.
  - A Troll of Stemb&auml;r with above-average HP can reach 204+ HP, allowing
    them to survive one melee attack, but not one Deadly Flash. However, their
    lower Luck prevents them from reaching WC.
- Each Fire Troll has a 50-50 chance to either use a regular attack, or cast Air
  to Fire.
  - The regular attack hits one target in the front two rows, but can be blocked
    with WC 15, which is possible for all Fighter, Knight, and Hunters, and even
    a Thief if he uses Parry. It has a 1/16 chance to hit anyway, so you will
    take an average of 0.8 hits in the first round, and at 74-77 damage a hit,
    any average hero can survive at least one hit.
  - Air to Fire is more dangerous, because it hits the whole party, including
    the spellcasters on the back row. However, you only need WC 9 to block it,
    which is attainable by any character.
- Offensively, the fastest way to kill enemies are the spells Medusas Eye (kills
  one group) and Deadly Flash (kills one individual). Your back row consists of
  Magicians/Wizards who can cast these spells, and the sole purpose of the rest
  of the party is to protect them.

The party therefore looks like this

Fighter, top left
: WC 17 (+3 Luck, +3 Magic Armour spell, +4 Fire Shield, +3 Knight Armour,
+3 Battle Helmet, +1 Killersword). Parry every round to maintain an effective
WC 20.

Hunter, top right
: WC 17 (+3 Luck, +3 Magic Armour spell, +4 Ara's Shield, +3 Ara's Armour,
+2 Arc's Helmet, +1 Arc's Sword, +1 Bee-Ring). Parry every round.

Thief, center left
: WC 13 (+3 Luck, +3 Magic Armour spell, +3 Buckler, +2 Sefer's Helmet,
+2 Armour, Bolas or Sword). There are better classes for the final fight, but
realistically this is what you have in your party. A good candidate to use
Anti-Aura scroll on turn 1, if you want to do it all in one go.
If one of the front row dies, this spot is promoted to the front row, where only
Parry will protect him from the melee attack of Fire Trolls.
Cannot survive a hit from the Dark Lord, although with some prepration you can
make your thief a Stemb&auml;r and carefully level him to have 204+ HP.
You could give him a scroll of New Live to resurrect the Healer or casters if
necessary) and Stemberfang (28-51 damage on the Dark Lord), but the main use of
the Thief is really just to pad out the front row so Deadly Flash can't hit the
casters.

Healer, center right
: WC 10 (+3 Luck, +3 Magic Armour spell, +1 Healing Robe, +1 Wood Shield,
+1 Helmet, +1 Arc's Boolas). At this WC, Parry won't make a difference.
Restoration is marginally useful to allow a Stemb&auml;r in slot 3 to
recover from a Dark Lord melee hit on the front row.
New Live is of limited use, as resurrected heroes return at the back of the
party where they can't do much.
Actually, the most useful thing might be to attack the Dark Lord with Arc's
Boolas for a little addditional damage.

Magician, back left
: WC 10 (+3 Luck, +3 Magic Armour spell, +1 Healing Robe, +1 Wood Shield,
+1 Helmet, +1 Arc's Boolas). Before the battle, cast Magic Armour to give
everyone WC +3. On the first round, cast Medusas Eye to kill the second stack of
Fire Trolls. On every subsequent round, cast Deadly Flash against the Dark Lord.
It deals 240 - 267 damage (average ~303), and the Dark Lord has 999 HP, so three
or four hits should do it.

Wizard, back right
: WC 10 (+3 Luck, +3 Magic Armour spell, +1 Healing Robe, +1 Wood Shield,
+1 Helmet, +1 Arc's Boolas). On the first round, cast Medusas Eye to kill the
third stack of Fire Trolls. (Third stack is larger, and the sixth slot is safer
because he won't enter Deadly Flash range until two heroes are killed.)
On every subsequent round, cast Deadly Flash against the Dark Lord.

If you are using the default party, i.e. you only have a Wizard and no Magician,
the fight will be more difficult:

- Wizard can only cast Magic Armour2, which only grants WC +2, so only one of
  your front row can have high enough WC to parry the Dark Lord.
- On average, Wynder at Level 16 has exactly enough MP (119) to cast Magic
  Armour2 once, Medusas Eye once, and Deadly Flash three times. On average, he
  has enough MP (117) to reliably survive three Air to Fire.
- Wynder should be placed in the bottom-right slot (safest), and cast Deadly
  Flash on the Dark Lord on the first three turns. This will kill him if you are
  lucky, or your allies can also land hits. On the fourth turn, cast Magic
  Armour on the stack of 8 Fire Trolls.
- Place Robin in the first slot and give him Arc's Sword. On average, he will
  reach Level 16 with higher Strength than Amber (118 vs 84), dealing 83-97
  damage with Arc's Sword. On average, three Deadly Flash and one Arc's Sword
  hit is exactly enough to kill the Dark Lord. Give Amber both the Battle Helmet
  and Bee-Ring so he can parry with WC 20 and hold the line.
- The monk Simarhon should mainly use Stormfist (20-37 damage to all enemies) or
  Restoration. However, attacking with Arc's Boolas will do more damage to a
  single target (33-44). The Healer is worth using New Live because it will help
  to have as many heroes as possible as a last resort to fight the last Fire
  Trolls.
- The battle is still very RNG, and may take multiple attempts. Your chance of
  surviving a round without losing a hero drops from about 70% to about 1/3.
  Wynder enters the Dark Lord's Deadly Flash range after two kills. Once
  the Fighter and Thief are gone, the Healer or Monk reach the front row and
  will be quickly killed by the Fire Trolls; the hero will on average take 3
  hits, and be killed by 2 or 3.
  The ~25% chance for the top-left hero to go before the Dark Lord in the first
  round is more important, and refreshing the encounter after using the
  Anti-Aura scroll is a useful strategy.

### Notes

* Avoid the southmost chest in the west treasure room, and the second chest
  from the south in the east treasure room. They are trapped and empty.
  The second chest from the south in the west room is also trapped, but
  contains the weapon Stemberfang.
* There are no rest points on this level. There are two in the level below,
  in the northeast and northwest of the map respectively.
* The unused questions simply read "Riddle 00" and "Solution 00" in German.
  No question squares appear on this level.
