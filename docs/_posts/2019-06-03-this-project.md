_Exploring the Dungeons of Avalon_ is a project to disassemble and analyze the
executable and data files of the 1991 Commodore Amiga roleplaying game _Dungeons
of Avalon_, and its 1992 sequel _Dungeons of Avalon II: The Island of Darkness_.

It also intends to research the mysterious origins of the game's publication and
translation into English.

It follows a similar project, [Exploring
K240](https://tetracorp.github.io/k240/), which performed an analysis of the
1994 space sim _K240_.

### What can we learn?

This project primarily intends to discover the following:

1. Can _Dungeons of Avalon 1_ be completed?
  - Due to a [bug](../game/bugs.html), the final boss cannot be killed. However,
    the cause has been discovered, and a four-byte hex edit fix is available.
    The [DoA1 ending](../game/doa1-ending.html) is now viewable.
2. What are the game's mechanics?
  - A more thorough analysis of the game disassembly has now uncovered the
    secrets of all the primary game mechanics, including 
    [character class](../game/character-class.html),
    [character creation and level up](../game/character-creation-and-level.html),
    [combat mechanics](../game/combat-mechanics.html),
    [dungeon format](../data/dungeon-format.html),
    [items and treasure](../game/items-and-treasure.html),
    [monster encounters](../game/monster-encounters.html),
    [race and class](../game/race-and-class.html),
    [save game format](../data/save-game-format.html),
    [spells](../game/spells.html),
    [traps](../game/traps.html),
    and [monster statistics](../game/monster-statistics.html).
3. What is the complete game map?
  - Maps for every level of both games are available, beginning with
   [DoA1 dungeon level 1](../maps/doa1-dungeon1.html). They include full lists of
   chest contents, monster encounter rates, and other notes.
4. When was the game released and translated into English?
 - The history has been researched detailed in an article on
   [development and release history](../history/development-and-release.html).

### History

This site was started on 3 June 2019.

In the first month, the project added full maps for each level, translations of
information from the German manual, lists of things like items and traps,
details of the game's ending, a partly-annotated disassembly of the German
version of _Dungeons of Avalon II_.

Occasional updates were made between 2020 and 2022. In 2024, the monster
graphics and other graphics were ripped.

In August 2026, a new disassembly of _Dungeons of Avalon_ began, revealing
numerous other mechanics. These include the effects of spells, more bytes of the
save game file, random encounter tables, full monster statistics, flee chance,
combat mechanics, item statistics.
