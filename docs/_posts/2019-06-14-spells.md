The German game manual for _Dungeons of Avalon_ contains spell lists for all
classes which can cast spells. For convenience, I've compiled them all into a
single list and cross-checked them with game data.

You can learn a new level of spells from The Wise Three at every two levels
after the first; i.e. 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13, and 15.

### Spell effects

Of 46 spells, there are 27 attack spells, 9 healing, 8 beneficial or utility
spells for out-of-combat use, and one unused (Teleport).

Attack spells deal a certain amount of damage in a range. They are otherwise
differentiated by their target type:

- One target, which hits one creature
- Group, which hits every monster in one stack of enemies
- All, which hits all enemies in groups

Some spells have a special effect worth noting:

Anti-Aura
: A single-target attack spell, but instead of dealing damage, it removes
the Dark Lord's anti-magic aura. The aura dismissal effect remains even if you
flee the battle, or save and load the game.

Birds View / Eagles View
: Automapping. Clicking the icon gives a top-down view. Any square you have ever
stood on or moved adjacent to will appear on your map, even if the spell wasn't
active at that time. The difference between the two spells is that Bird's View
shows limited detail: passable squares as empty space, solid squares as wall,
and doors normally. (See [dungeon format](../data/dungeon-format.html) for a
list of square types.) Eagle's View shows squares in detail, including stairs,
hidden doors, etc. Duration of 0 in both cases.

Deadly Flash
: Instant kill single-target spell. It actually does this by just having a
damage value high enough to kill most things. Although internally 240 - 480
damage, the random damage variance roll for the playeris capped at 127, so
really 127 - 367 damage. When a monster uses it, it hits for 240 - 482.

Killmagic
: Disappears forcefields. DoA2 only.

Levitation / Levitation II
: Immunity to ice floor, but not pit traps. Levitation spells are actually coded
to give you an attack bonus, but both spells have a 0 for the bonus given. Aside
from MP cost, the only difference is duration: Levitation is 2, while Levitation
II is 5.

Magic Armour
: Adds +3 armour to the party for a duration of 5.

Magic Armour2
: Adds +2 armour to the party for a duration of 3.

Magic Eye
: Clicking the icon gives you your exact coordinates in the dungeon. Duration 2.

Medusas Eye
: Instant-kill group spell. Group-target version of Deadly Flash.

New Live
: Returns a dead hero to life with exactly 6 HP.

Restoration
: Full heals the party.

Teleport
: Unavailable either as a scroll or spell.

### Manual errors

The German game manual actually has some errors:

1. All attack spells used by a hero actually have a 3 point bonus to the upper
  bound which is not listed in the manual. This article includes those bonuses.
  Note that when monsters cast spells, they only has add a +2 bonus to upper
  bound.
2. Heilung III (Healing III) heals 4-9, not 5-19. It is effectively Healing I
  but for the whole party.
3. The DoA1 manual incorrectly lists Flame Speer (Flammenspeer) as targeting
  Group, when it targets One. The DoA2 manual corrects the manual.
4. The DoA2 manual incorrectly lists Frost Breath (Eisatem) as targeting One,
  and costing 10 MP. In both games it targets Group and costs 18 MP. The
  manual's editor probably confused Eisatem for Eisstarre.
5. In the _Dungeons of Avalon_ manual only, Stemberfang is inconsistently listed
  as having a target of "All" in the Magician spell list, but "Group" in the
  Wizard spell list. It is correctly Group.

### Trivia

1. All spells have the same mana (MP) cost regardless of which class casts them.
  However, some classes get a spell at a lower level than others.
2. Anti-Aura and Killmagic can only be found as scrolls.
3. Teleport appears to be unavailable. It does not appear on the spell list for
   any class, and there is no scroll of Teleport in the item list.
4. Scrolls of nine spells can be found in _Dungeons of Avalon_:
  Anti-Aura (1), Antipoisen (3), Eaglefang (1), Eagles View (1), Magic Eye (3),
  New Live (1), Restoration (3), Stemberfang (1), and Wasp Sting (2).
5. Scrolls of 17 spells can be found in _Dungeons of Avalon II_:
  Anti-Aura (1), Antipoisen (3), Birds View (3), Deadly Flash (7),
  Eaglefang (2), Eagles View (3), Healing II (4), Ice Breath (1),
  Killmagic (16), Levitation (8), Magic Armour (4), Magic Eye (4), New Live (3),
  Restone (4), Restoration(3), Stemberfang (1), and Wasp Sting (2).
6. Killmagic is used in _Dungeons of Avalon II_ to disable magic walls
  (forcefields). These magic walls take up block slot 0x15, previously used in
  _Dungeons of Avalon_ for loose stone walls.
  They still appear with a wall texture on the Eagles View map for this reason.
  There are 11 magic walls in _Dungeons of Avalon II_, but a total of 16 scrolls
  of Killmagic appear in chests.
7. All classes have exactly two spells per level. The exception is in the first
  _Dungeons of Avalon_, where the Magician and Wizard have only one spell each
  of level 8.
8. Two unused spell slots appear in DoA1. Both have the name &ast;&ast;&ast;
  and are unobainable in game, but still have stats row.
  - Spell `0e` would cost 22 MP, have unused spell type `03`, and a target of
    All/Party. The description field of `01` references the unused spell
    text "THE PARTY WAS TELEPORTED." The slot would later be used in DoA2 for
    Killmagic.
  - Spell `10` would cost 18 MP, and be an Attack spell with target Group, but
    no damage. The slot was used in DoA2 by Teleport.
9. In the data, the Restoration spell has heal values of 4-9, the same as Healing
  I and Healing III. However, they are unused, as Restoration simply fills HP to
  maximum.

### Changes in Dungeons of Avalon II

_Dungeons of Avalon II_ makes the following changes:

1. Three new spells are added:
   Disarm Trap (Ent. Falle),
   Killmagic (Magiekiller), and
   Teleport.
2. The Healer gets Disarm Trap as a level 7 spell instead of
   Eagles View.
3. The Magician gets Disarm Trap as an additional level 8 spell.
4. The Wizard also gets Disarm Trap as an additional level 8 spell.
   With this, all classes have exactly two spells of each level.
5. In German, the armour spells are confusingly renamed. Magier Rüstung becomes
   Hexer Rüstung, while Hexer Rüstung becomes Hexer Rüstung2. In DoA1, they were
   named for their respective classes (Magier = Magician, Hexer = Wizard).
6. Frosty Fist's German name is changed from Eisstarre to Frostfaust.
7. Internally, all non-combat utility spells are now given the type `FF`. In
   DoA1, Eagles View and Birds View had type Group and the others had type
   All. Erroneously, Frosty Fist had type `FF` in DoA1, which is corrected to
   Group in DoA2.

### Internals

For interest, spells are stored in a table like this:

- 00: Spell ID number
- 01: MP cost. Same for every class
- 02: Beneficial effects for party utility spells or cure status spells:
  - 00: Magic Eye (party magic)
  - 01: Magic Armour (party magic)
  - 02: Levitation (party magic)
  - 03: Birds View (party magic)
  - 10: Cure Blindness (cure status)
  - 40: Cure Stoned (cure status)
  - 80: Cure Poisoned (cure status)
- 03: Effect type:
  - 00: Party utility spells
  - 01: Attack
  - 02: Healing (HP)
  - 03: Disarm trap (DoA2 only)
  - 04: Cure status
  - 05: New Live
  - 06: Restoration
  - 07: Killmagic (DoA2 only)
  - 08: Teleport (DoA2 only)
- 04: Target:
  - 00: All (all enemies, or all party)
  - 01: Group
  - 02: Single target (one enemy, or one ally)
  - FF: Party utility spells
- 05: Base damage or spell duration
- 06: Variable damage range (actually 3 higher)
- 07: Message text ID
